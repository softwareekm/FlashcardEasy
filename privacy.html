2) What data we collect

FlashCardEasy is designed to work without creating an account. We do not require sign-up and we do not collect personal data through app accounts.

  • Local app data: Your flashcards, lists, and related content are stored locally on your device. We do not automatically upload this content to our servers.
  • No analytics / no crash reporting: We do not use Firebase Crashlytics (or similar) to collect crash reports, and we do not collect analytics or diagnostics from users.

3) Why we process data

App content is stored locally only to provide core functionality (creating, editing, and using your flashcards inside the app).

4) Third-party services

FlashCardEasy does not use third-party analytics or crash-reporting services to collect user data.

5) Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data for advertising purposes. Since user content is stored locally, it is not routinely shared with us.

6) Data retention

Your flashcard data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app.

7) Your choices and deletion

You can delete your flashcards directly within the app (if such feature is available) or by uninstalling the app from your device. If you have privacy questions, contact us at software.ekm@gmail.com.

8) Children’s privacy

FlashCardEasy is not specifically directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9) International users

The app may be available worldwide. Because data is stored locally on-device, cross-border transfer by us is generally not involved in normal app usage.

10) Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page with a new effective date.
