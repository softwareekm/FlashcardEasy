Privacy Policy

Vocabulary Card

Effective date: 2026-01-17

Summary

  • No ads.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We use crash reports/diagnostics to improve stability.

In the app you may see the message: “We use analytics to improve the app.” This refers to diagnostics/crash reporting (Crashlytics) used to fix errors and improve reliability.

1) Who we are

Developer: EKM Software
Contact email: software.ekm@gmail.com

2) What data we collect

Vocabulary Card is designed to work without creating an account. We store your word lists locally on your device.

  • Diagnostics (Crash reports): When the app crashes or encounters certain errors, we may collect diagnostic information such as crash stack traces, device/OS information, and app version. This data is processed through Firebase Crashlytics (Google).
  • Local app data: Your word lists and words are stored locally on your device (offline storage). We do not automatically upload them to our servers.

3) Why we collect this data

  • To detect, investigate, and fix crashes and stability issues.
  • To improve app reliability and user experience.

4) Third-party services

We use Firebase Crashlytics, a service provided by Google, to collect crash reports and diagnostics. You can learn more about Google/Firebase privacy practices here: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

5) Data sharing

We do not sell your data. Crash/diagnostic data is processed by our service provider (Google Firebase Crashlytics) for the purpose of crash reporting.

6) Data retention

Crash reports are retained for as long as needed to analyze and fix issues, subject to Crashlytics/Firebase retention settings. Local word list data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app.

7) Your choices and requests (including deletion)

Since we do not require accounts, there is no “account deletion” process inside the app. If you want to request deletion of crash/diagnostic data to the extent possible, contact us at: software.ekm@gmail.com

To help us locate relevant data, you may include (optional): device model, Android/iOS version, and approximate date/time of the issue.

8) Children’s privacy

Vocabulary Card is not specifically directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9) International users

The app is available worldwide. Data may be processed by our service providers (e.g., Google Firebase) in accordance with their systems and policies.

10) Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page with a new effective date.

11) Contact
